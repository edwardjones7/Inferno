File mapping (renamed copies; the .m4a files are mono AAC re-encodes made
with ffmpeg from the original .ogg files, for browsers without Vorbis):

  card-deal-1..6   <- card-slide-1..6.ogg
  card-flip-1..4   <- card-place-1..4.ogg
  chip-stack-1..6  <- chips-stack-1..6.ogg
  chip-clack-1..4  <- chips-collide-1..4.ogg
  chip-lay-1..3    <- chip-lay-1..3.ogg

All other sounds in the app (win/lose stingers, fire, ambience, etc.) are
synthesized at runtime in src/lib/sound.ts and use no audio files.
